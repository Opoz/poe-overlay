# Third-party data and notices

## exile-leveling

Campaign route files and area data in `crates/poe-core/data/exile-leveling/` come from [exile-leveling](https://github.com/HeartofPhos/exile-leveling) by HeartofPhos, used under the MIT License (full text in that folder's `LICENSE`; upstream commit in `SOURCE_COMMIT`). The Guide parser that turns them into per-Area Steps is our own.

`crates/poe-core/data/quests.json` is generated by `scripts/gen-quests.mjs` from exile-leveling's quest data (hand-in NPCs, MIT) and [repoe-fork](https://github.com/repoe-fork/repoe-fork.github.io)'s base items (book names).

## Passive tree data

`crates/poe-core/data/tree.json` is generated by `scripts/gen-tree.mjs` from:

- [grindinggear/skilltree-export](https://github.com/grindinggear/skilltree-export): GGG's official passive tree export (layout, names, connections).
- [repoe-fork](https://github.com/repoe-fork/repoe-fork.github.io): maps the passive ids printed in the Client Log to tree nodes.

`crates/poe-core/data/atlas.json` is generated by `scripts/gen-atlas.mjs` from [grindinggear/atlastree-export](https://github.com/grindinggear/atlastree-export), GGG's official atlas tree export.

## Zone layouts

The app ships no zone layout images. Players add their own to the layouts folder.

## poelab

Lab layouts come from [poelab.com](https://www.poelab.com/): the player opens the site in their own browser and copies the day's compass file. Nothing is downloaded from poelab automatically. Please support poelab by allowing its ads.
